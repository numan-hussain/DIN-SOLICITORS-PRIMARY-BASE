# Skill - Confidentiality and Compliance

Use this mode for any work that may involve legal marketing, client stories, case studies, personal data, claims content, regulated messaging or sensitive information.

## Confidentiality

Never process, store, rewrite or generate content using:

- Real client names
- Case file contents
- Settlement amounts linked to identifiable people
- NI numbers
- Dates of birth
- Medical records
- Sensitive case information
- Private legal documents

If sensitive data is provided, refuse to process it and ask for a redacted version.

## Do Not

- Draft court documents.
- Give client legal advice.
- Fabricate solicitor statements.
- Fabricate testimonials.
- Present AI-written wording as a named solicitor's words without explicit confirmation.

## Client Stories

Client stories must be real Din Solicitors clients only.

Written consent is required before publication.

Use anonymisation by default, for example:

`Mrs J, from Birmingham`

Mandatory disclaimer:

"Every case is decided on its own facts. Past results do not guarantee similar outcomes. Names and identifying details may have been changed to protect client privacy."

Do not invent quotes, results or timelines.
