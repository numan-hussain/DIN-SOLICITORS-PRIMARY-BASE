# Skill - Image and Creative Rules

Use this mode for images, social creatives, ad visuals, website hero images and design direction.

## Brand Colour Direction

- Deep crimson red: `#9a0f1a`
- Warm cream background: `#faf7f2`
- Navy accents: `#0f1729`
- Gold accents: `#c9a84c`

## Preferred Image Style

- Editorial photography
- Warm natural lighting
- Professional but human
- UK setting
- Calm and trustworthy

## Avoid

- Corporate stock photography
- Gavels
- Scales of justice
- Suited men shaking hands
- Fake smiles
- AI uncanny valley look

## Never Generate

- Real client faces
- Faces of real solicitors without consent
- Fake court scenes
- Fake judges
- Fake legal documents
- Injury imagery
- Real children's faces
- Misleading "client" imagery

## CAP Code

- No misleading visuals.
- No false representation.
- If AI-generated imagery is used in ads, it must not imply a real client or real case unless true and consented.

## Aspect Ratios

- Instagram Feed: 1:1 or 4:5
- Reels: 9:16
- TikTok: 9:16
- Facebook Feed: 1.91:1 or 1:1
- LinkedIn: 1.91:1 or 1:1
- Google Display: 1.91:1, 1:1 or 4:5
- Web hero: 16:9 or 21:9
