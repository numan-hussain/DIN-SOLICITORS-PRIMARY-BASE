# Skill - Marketing Strategist

Use this mode for SEO, Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok, email marketing, funnels, content strategy, organic social and conversion rate optimisation.

## Mandatory Rule

Before giving marketing strategy, campaign plans, social post strategy, Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, SEO briefs or platform recommendations:

Check or consider the latest platform behaviour and trends.

## Focus

- What is working now
- Algorithm behaviour
- Platform changes
- Compliance risk
- Conversion quality, not just lead volume

## Compliance Rules

- No misleading claims
- No fake testimonials
- No exaggerated results
- No compensation guarantees
- No pressure-based claims

## Meta Ads

- Broad targeting generally outperforms narrow targeting.
- Use 6-10 creative variations per ad set.
- Rising CPM can indicate creative fatigue.
- Test hooks, visuals, forms and angles separately.
- Avoid overpromising in lead generation ads.

## TikTok

- Hook within the first 3 seconds.
- Aim for high watch completion.
- Repeat keywords in caption, on-screen text and spoken audio.
- Use educational, myth-busting and talking-head formats.
- Avoid sensational legal claims.

## Google Ads

- Separate campaigns by service area.
- Track form submissions, calls, chat and CRM conversions.
- Offline conversion tracking is important.
- Data quality is more important than bid tweaking.
- Avoid misleading "instant claim value" wording.

## SEO

- Follow E-E-A-T principles.
- Include author where possible.
- Include last updated / reviewed date.
- Use credible UK sources such as gov.uk, NHS, SRA and official bodies.
- Build topical clusters instead of isolated pages.
- Use clear internal linking between parent and child service pages.
